August 10, 2024 Multiple Speakers MIT Lecture Hall

Jet Aerospace

As part of the Industry Skill Development initiatives, a guest lecture was conducted on Jet Aerospace, offering students valuable insights into the latest developments in the field. The session covered topics ranging from advanced jet propulsion systems to the latest trends in aerospace manufacturing and maintenance. Students gained practical knowledge from industry expertise, enhancing their understanding and preparing them for future careers in the aerospace sector.

September 20, 2024 Dr. Rajesh Kumar, ISRO MIT Auditorium

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)

The Association of Aeronautical Engineers is proud to host a special guest talk by one of our distinguished alumni, who is currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the United States of America. The session will focus on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) of multiphase flows, with applications in both biomedical engineering and manufacturing domains. Alongside his research insights, the speaker will also introduce students to Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), USA, providing valuable exposure to international research and academic opportunities. The talk is scheduled for 45–50 minutes and is primarily aimed at students from the 3rd and 4th year, who possess a background in CFD. However, 1st and 2nd year students are also welcome to attend, as the session will be kept as general and accessible as possible.

Multiple Sessions Mr.Chelian N G, Chairperson, AAE Live classes

GATE Classes

This lecture series is designed to help students prepare for the GATE exam, covering key topics in aerospace engineering and related fields. Mr. Chelian N G will guide participants through the syllabus, providing valuable tips and resources for effective study.

July 18, 2025 Mr.Chelian N G, Chairperson, AAE Classroom

Session On Introduction To GATE Classes And Overview Of NPTEL Courses

The Session on Introduction to GATE and Overview of NPTEL Courses was held on 18th July 2025 at LHC 109. This informative session will provide students with clarity on the GATE exam structure, effective preparation strategies, and the benefits of attempting GATE. It will also offer insights into NPTEL courses and how they can enhance academic learning while supporting career growth. Participants will receive guidance on early planning, time management, and aligning their academic journey with long-term career aspirations. With inputs from experienced mentors and opportunities to clarify doubts related to higher studies and certifications, this session is a valuable opportunity for students.
