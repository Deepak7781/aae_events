FLIGHT 2025

Pushing the boundaries of aerospace innovation. An overview of the flagship symposium's events and competitions.

Back to Events

Date

August 25-27, 2025

Location

MIT Campus, Auditorium

Attendees

Students, Faculty, Industry Experts

FLIGHT 2025 was the Association of Aeronautical Engineers' premier annual symposium, bringing together the brightest minds in aerospace engineering. This year's theme, "Sustainable Skies: The Future of Flight", focused on environmentally conscious design, alternative propulsion, and advanced materials. The event included keynote addresses from global leaders, hands-on workshops, and a prestigious design competition.

Workshops & Competitions

Competition Prize Pool Team Event

Drone Racing Event

A thrilling competition where participants race their custom-built drones through a complex, multi-level obstacle course. The event tests not only the speed and agility of the drones but also the pilots' precision and fast-paced decision-making skills. The competition highlights the importance of aerodynamics, power systems, and control systems in an exciting, high-stakes format.

Competition Intermediate Level

RC Plane Competition

This competition challenges participants to design, build, and fly a remote-controlled aircraft to complete a series of aerobatic and endurance tasks. Judged on flight performance, design efficiency, and stability, this event is a true test of aeromodelling expertise. It provides an excellent opportunity for aspiring engineers to apply theoretical knowledge of aerodynamics and structural design in a practical setting.

Competition Beginner Friendly

Glider Modelling & Competition

Participants are introduced to the fundamental principles of glider design, aerodynamics, and lightweight construction. This hands-on workshop is followed by a competition where teams launch their hand-built gliders, with the winning team determined by the longest flight duration. The event fosters creativity and a deep understanding of flight physics without the complexity of powered flight.

Presentation Public Speaking

Paper Presentation

This event serves as a premier platform for students, researchers, and professionals to present their latest findings and innovative ideas in aerospace engineering. Topics range from propulsion systems and advanced materials to sustainable aviation and space exploration. It's an opportunity to engage in intellectual discourse, receive feedback from experts, and contribute to the aerospace community.

Competition Case Study

Aircrash Investigation

A simulation-based workshop where teams step into the shoes of aviation accident investigators. They are given a detailed scenario, including flight data, cockpit voice recordings, and evidence from the crash site. Teams must use their knowledge of aerospace systems, human factors, and forensic analysis to determine the cause of the simulated crash and propose safety recommendations.

Workshop Hands-on Experience

Drone Workshop

An intensive, hands-on workshop designed to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of drone technology. Starting with the fundamental principles of flight, participants move on to the assembly of a real-time drone, including soldering, programming, and calibration. The workshop culminates in a supervised test flight, giving participants a complete, practical experience.

Workshop Programming & Electronics

Avionics Workshop

This workshop delves into the world of aircraft electronics, teaching participants the basics of ADA programming and its application in aviation systems. Participants gain practical experience working with Arduino boards to build and program simple avionics components, such as flight control indicators and sensor systems. It's an excellent introduction to embedded systems in an aerospace context.

Workshop Beginner Friendly

Basics of Flight

Tailored for first- and second-year students, this introductory workshop demystifies the principles of flight. Topics covered include the four forces of flight (lift, thrust, drag, weight), airfoil design, and the basics of aircraft stability and control. The session uses interactive models and simple experiments to make complex concepts easy to understand and engaging.

Workshop Materials & Fabrication

Composite Workshop

A two-day workshop on the use of composite materials in aerospace engineering. Participants learn about different types of composites, their manufacturing processes, and testing methods. The hands-on session focuses on fabrication techniques like lay-up and vacuum bagging, giving students practical experience in creating strong, lightweight materials essential for modern aircraft and spacecraft.

Competition Beginner Friendly

Bottle Rocketry

A fun and competitive event where teams design and construct water bottle rockets. The workshop covers the basics of propulsion, stability, and trajectory. Participants then compete to launch their rockets for maximum altitude and flight time, learning key principles of rocketry in an accessible and exciting way.

Workshop Model Rocketry

Rocket Building Workshop

This workshop introduces participants to the fundamentals of model rocketry, including design considerations for propulsion, aerodynamics, and stability. Teams work together to build their own model rockets from a kit, learning about the various components like the nose cone, fins, and engine. The workshop concludes with a launch day to test their creations.

Workshop Hands-on Aeromodelling

RC Plane Building

A hands-on workshop focused on the practical construction of remote-controlled planes. Participants learn about structural materials, electronics integration, and control surface setup. The collaborative environment allows for peer learning and mentorship, culminating in a successful maiden flight of their self-built aircraft.

Workshop Mechanical Engineering

IC Engines Workshop

An in-depth exploration of Internal Combustion (IC) Engines, covering their fundamental working principles, different types (e.g., piston, jet), and their application in aviation. The workshop provides theoretical insights alongside practical demonstrations, including the disassembly and reassembly of a small engine to understand its components and mechanisms.

Workshop Live Demonstration

Jet Handling

This workshop offers an exclusive opportunity to gain an in-depth understanding of turbojet engines and their applications. The session covers the theory of jet propulsion and culminates in a live firing demonstration of a scaled-down jet engine. Participants learn about the components of a jet engine and the immense power they generate.

Workshop Software & Simulation

ANSYS Workshop

An advanced workshop focused on enhancing skills in analyzing engineering components using ANSYS Workbench. This session teaches participants how to perform structural, thermal, and fluid dynamics simulations. It is a critical skill for modern aerospace design, allowing engineers to test concepts virtually before physical prototyping.
