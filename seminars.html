Past Seminars

Januaury 8, 2025 Mr.Sidesh Karthik, Indian AirForce Seminar Hall, Aerospace Department

Career Opportunities in Indian AirForce

On 8th January 2025, the Association of Aeronautical Engineers, the official student body of the Department of Aerospace Engineering, MIT Campus, organised a session focusing on the career opportunities in the prestigious Indian Air Force, which was resourced by a distinguished Alumnus Group Captain Mr. Sidesh Karthik, Surya Kiran acrobatics Team, Indian Air Force in the Seminar Hall of the Department of Aerospace Engineering, MIT Campus, Anna University. He was welcomed warmly with a shawl and a special token of gratitude by Prof. K. Ravichandran, Dean of MIT Campus, Dr. K.M. Paramasivam, Head of the Department of Aerospace Engineering and Dr. S. Ilakkiya, Assistant Professor, Department of Aerospace Engineering.

October 15, 2024 Multiple Speakers Rajam Hall

Youth Awakening Day Seminar

On 15 October 2024, the Department of Aerospace Engineering at MIT Campus celebrated Youth Awakening Day on the 93rd birth anniversary of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam with a one-day Technical Seminar that brought together an eminent panel of leaders from academia, industry, and research. Distinguished guests included Prof. Dr. R. Velraj, Former Vice-Chancellor of Anna University, Shri Renganathan, Director, Murugappa Group, and Shri N. Ramachandran, MD, MEL Systems & Services Ltd. and President of AIDAT - all of whom were warmly felicitated by HOD Dr. K.M. Paramasivam and the Dean, MIT Campus. The seminar featured thought-provoking sessions by Dr. S. Ganesan, Scientist G, GTRE, DRDO, Dr. R. Kalimuthu, Group Director, ISRO, Mahendra Giri, and Dr. Dipankar Das, VSSC, Trivandrum, each of whom was also honoured for their contributions. A significant highlight of the day was the signing of an MoU with Ladakh University, marking a new chapter of collaboration, with Prof. Dr. Ibrahim present for the occasion. Dr. S. Mehta joined the celebrations online, adding to the spirit of knowledge exchange and innovation that defined the event.

May 3 & 4, 2024 Multiple Speakers Rajam Hall

Two Day National Level Technical Seminar

The two-day National Level Technical Seminar on Emerging Technologies in Aerospace Development, held on May 3-4, 2024, was a vibrant mix of innovation, expertise, and inspiration. The event took off with the presence of Dr. Sanjay Kumar Pandey, Secretary, ARDB & DRDO, New Delhi, who was warmly felicitated by the Registrar and Dean, MIT Campus. Over the course of two days, the seminar became a hub of cutting-edge discussions, where industry experts, researchers, and students explored breakthroughs shaping the future of aerospace technology. Day 2 carried an essence of nostalgia as retired professors were honoured with shawls and mementoes for their lifelong dedication to the field. The seminar concluded with an engaging valedictory ceremony, leaving participants inspired and eager to translate these ideas into real-world innovation.
