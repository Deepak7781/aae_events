Past Industrial Visits

Date IAF Tambaram, Tamil Nadu

Tambaram AirForce Station Visit

As part of the industrial exposure activities of the Association of Aeronautical Engineers, an enriching visit to the Air Force Station was organised, offering students a rare glimpse into the nation’s aerial defence domain. The young visitors received a warm and inspiring welcome, followed by a dedicated session on career opportunities in the Indian Air Force, covering eligibility criteria, entrance examinations, service tenure, and pathways to join this elite force. The officers encouraged questions from the students, patiently addressing their curiosities and sharing valuable guidance. The highlight of the visit was the exclusive tour of the massive hangars, where students stood in awe of the trainer aircraft and fighter jets stationed within, learning in detail about their design, capabilities, and operational roles. This first-hand experience deepened their technical understanding and ignited the dream of wearing the IAF uniform among many in the student community.

October 1, 2024 IAF Tambaram, Tamil Nadu

Induction Publicity Exhibition Vehicle

The Induction Publicity Exhibition Vehicle (IPEV) is a unique initiative by the IAF to promote awareness about the Air Force and its career opportunities among the youth. The IPV is equipped with state-of-the-art audio-visual aids and interactive displays, making it an effective tool for outreach and engagement. During the visit, students had the chance to explore the IPV, interact with IAF personnel, and gain insights into the various roles within the Air Force.On 01 October 2024, the Induction Publicity Exhibition Vehicle from IAF Tambaram visited the MIT Campus, drawing enthusiastic participation. The interactive session offered a glimpse into the Indian Air Force’s cutting-edge technology, operational capabilities, and rich legacy of service. Officers engaged with students to discuss career prospects in the IAF, highlighting the diverse roles, training opportunities, and various privileges. The visit not only showcased the pride and professionalism of the Air Force but also ignited curiosity and ambition among the student community.

July 10, 2024 DRDO, Hyderabad Telangana, India

DRDO Aeronautics Lab Visit

This visit offered a deep dive into DRDO’s aeronautics research, focusing on missile and UAV development. Attendees toured testing facilities and learned about cutting-edge defense technologies shaping India’s aerospace capabilities.
