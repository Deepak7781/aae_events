Logo Design Competition

The Logo Design Competition invited Aeronautical Engineering students to create a unique identity for the Association of Aeronautical Engineers (AAE). The event received enthusiastic participation, with students submitting creative and symbolic designs that captured the spirit of the association. The AAE Design & Editing Team supported the initiative by designing the official competition poster, ensuring effective publicity and engagement.

Essay and Poster Competition

Held in honour of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, the Remembrance Anniversary competitions encouraged students to express their ideas through essays and creative poster designs. These events inspired participants to connect technical knowledge with creativity while paying tribute to the former President’s vision. The AAE Design & Editing Team contributed by creating impactful posters to promote and highlight the competitions.
