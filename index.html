History of Our Events

2025

+

AAE organized Flight 2025, our largest symposium yet, with over 500 attendees and international speakers. We also held a successful drone-building workshop.

2024

+

We celebrated our 10th anniversary with a special edition of Flight, and hosted a lecture series on the future of electric aviation.

2023

+

Our student team won the national Aero Design Challenge. This year also saw the introduction of our popular industrial visits program.
