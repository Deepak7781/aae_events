August 10, 2025 Speaker Name Seminar Hall, Aerospace Department

Drone Workshop

On August 09 2025, the Department of Aerospace Engineering, in collaboration with the Association of Aeronautical Engineers and Air Vulture Aerospace, hosted an engaging Career Takeoff Session on Drone Technology at LHC 109, MIT Campus. The event drew engineering students, tech enthusiasts, and curious learners eager to explore the fast-evolving world of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). The session opened with a warm welcome to industry experts and participants, creating an atmosphere of enthusiasm and curiosity. Speakers introduced attendees to the fundamentals of drone technology, showcasing its rapid advancements and applications in defence, logistics, agriculture, and environmental monitoring. The discussions highlighted the growing demand for skilled professionals in UAV design, avionics, and autonomous navigation. Experts provided valuable insights into emerging career opportunities, industry trends, and the technical skills required to excel in this dynamic field. Students actively participated in interactive Q&A segments, networking with mentors and exploring niche career paths. A key takeaway for attendees was gaining priority access to future hands-on workshops, ensuring continued practical exposure and learning. The session concluded with students motivated to contribute to the UAV revolution, viewing the event not only as an introduction but as a launchpad for innovation and growth.

06 August, 2025 Mr. Shrinath, AAE Design Team Classroom: LHC 005

Canva Workshop

In today’s digital world, design and presentation skills are as important as technical knowledge. To cater to this need, a hands-on training session on Canva was organized. Students were introduced to the essentials of visual design, including typography, layout, and color combinations. They gained practical experience in creating impactful posters, social media graphics, and professional presentations, skills that can be applied in academic projects, event promotions, and industry-related tasks.

August 13, 2025 Mr. Barath, AAE Design Team Classroom: LHC 005

PowerDirector Video Editing Workshop

Recognizing the growing importance of multimedia communication, specialized classes on PowerDirector video editing were arranged. These sessions provided students with exposure to video editing tools, techniques for adding transitions and effects, and methods for enhancing audio-visual quality. Students were also guided on structuring a storyline and producing creative video content, equipping them with skills highly relevant for project documentation, content creation, and professional presentations. Together, these sessions provided students with a holistic learning experience, bridging technical knowledge with creativity and digital proficiency. Such initiatives not only prepare students for academic and career opportunities but also nurture all-round skill development essential in today’s fast-evolving professional landscape.
